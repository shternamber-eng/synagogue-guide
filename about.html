About This Site

Synagogue Heritage Guide is an independent educational resource for Jewish communities, synagogue administrators, and rabbis worldwide.

Synagogue Heritage Guide was created to fill a gap: there is no shortage of Judaica retailers, but there are very few independent, in-depth educational resources specifically for synagogue administrators and community leaders.

Our articles cover the practical and spiritual dimensions of synagogue life — from the history of Jewish memorial traditions to practical guides for purchasing memorial boards, planning capital projects, and honoring community members.

Our Mission

To provide synagogue communities with high-quality, accurate, and practically useful information — free of charge — so that every congregation, regardless of size or resources, can serve its members well.

Editorial Approach

All articles on this site are written with care for both accuracy and accessibility. Where we reference Jewish law or tradition, we draw on established halachic sources. Where we offer practical recommendations, we draw on real experience with synagogue communities.
