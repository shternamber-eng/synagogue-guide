Lubavitch Heritage

The Village Behind the Name

Before Lubavitch became a global name, it was a real village.

The Village

Lubavitch is a historic village in Russia associated with Chabad-Lubavitch.

The Rebbeim

Lubavitch is associated with the Lubavitcher Rebbeim including the Tzemach Tzedek and Rebbe Maharash.

Torah and Chassidic Life

Tomchei Temimim and communal Torah life shaped this center.

Destruction, Silence, and Return

Many sites were damaged in the 20th century; preservation efforts returned later.

Lubavitch Today

Today it is a place of prayer, memory, and documentation.
